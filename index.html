    How Classroom SmartFind Works

    1. Report the Item

    Lost your calculator or found an ID card? Submit detailed marks and location.

    2. Smart Matching

    Our algorithm scores name, color, category, and classroom location to suggest matches.

    3. Submit Verification Claim

    Answer security questions to prove actual ownership of the item.

    4. Secure Classroom Handover

    Admin verifies the claim and marks the item returned once delivered.
